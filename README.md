**The Daily Globe** — the world news of Wednesday 25 November 2026, summed up in a single image drawn as a newspaper front page.

The lead story is Huawei's conviction in a U.S. federal criminal trial (guilty on 10 of 12 counts). Around it: the CIA cyber breach, Ukraine's strikes on Russian ships in Libya and the Gulf of Finland, the WHO evacuation of an Ebola hospital in Congo, the Sarkozy case sent to trial in Paris, and the Houthi drone strike on a Riyadh tower — plus an "Also Today" strip with the White House turkey pardon, U.S. consumer confidence, the World Chess Championship and the O'Hare radar outage. All stories were compiled from Reuters, AP and AFP reporting of 24–25 November 2026, and every picture on the page was drawn for this page.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

This writes `entry.png` (1024×1024) to the output directory. `generate_image.py` is the program that drew the front page; the PNG it produced is committed as `entry.png`, and `main.ts` publishes exactly those bytes.
